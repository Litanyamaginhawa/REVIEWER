Wellness

Defined wellness is the optimal health and vitality encompassing the dimensions of well-being. Wellness is relatively new concept that expand our idea of health and refers to living life to its fullest.

The Eight Dimension of Wellness

  1. Occupational Wellness
  2. Financial Wellness
  3. Environmental Wellness
  4. Intellectual Wellness
  5. Spiritual Wellness
  6. Social Wellness
  7. Emotional Wellness
  8. Physical Wellness

Physical Fitness

State of health and well being

  • Specifically the ability to perform aspects of sports
  • Achieved to proper nutrition
  • Moderate physical exercise
  • Sufficient rest

Hypokinetic Disease — Condition occur as a result of lack of exercise and movement

11 Components of Physical Fitness

Health Related Components: Cardiovascular Endurance, Muscular Strength, Muscular Endurance, Flexibility, Body Composition.

Skill Related Components: Agility, Coordination, Reaction time, Speed, Power, Balance.

7 Principles of Physical Training

1. Progression
2. Regularity
3. Overload
4. Variety
5. Recovery
6. Balance
7. Specificity

FITT: Frequency — How often; Intensity — How hard; Time — How long; Type — What kind

Circuit Training

Consists of a consecutive series of timed exercises performed one after the other, with varying amounts of rest between each exercise.

Circuit Training is an excellent way to simultaneously improve mobility, strength, and stamina.

An example of simple circuit training workout might consist of Push-ups, Sit-ups, Squats, Lunges and High Knees.

Types of Circuit Training

Timed circuit: working to a set time period for both rest and exercise intervals.

Competition Circuit: push yourself to see how many repetitions you can do.

Repetition Circuit: groups may do different repetitions according to level.

3 Types of Exercise

Warm Up Exercise — A period or act of preparation for a game, performance, or exercise session, involving gentle exercise.

Work Out Exercise — A practice or exercise to test or improve one’s fitness for athletic competition, ability or performance.

Cool Down Exercise — To allow the body to gradually transition to a resting or near-resting state after an intense exercise.

3 Types of Stretching

Static Stretching — executed by extending the targeted muscle group to its maximal point and holding it for 16 seconds or more.

Dynamic Stretching — active movements that stretch the muscles to their full range of motion.

Ballistic Stretching — utilizes repeated bouncing movement to stretch the targeted muscle group.
